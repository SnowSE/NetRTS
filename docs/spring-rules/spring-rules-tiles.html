BADGER BRAWL · SPRING 2027

The new rules

Less room on the map, grubs that grow back, and a score that counts what you own. Here is everything that changes.

01
Cells fill up
A cell holds 9 points. Worker 2, Soldier 1, Archer 1, Scout 2, Snow Mobile 9.
02
First in keeps the cell
Units walk through each other. Stop where it's full and you're bumped to the nearest cell with room.
03
Two dig per cell
Two workers dig from each cell beside a patch. Two more can wait their turn there.
04
Patches grow back
Slowly, faster when fuller, never past where they started. Dig one to zero and it's gone.
05
Compost Heaps
A farm: 100 to build, grows 300 grubs, dug 1 a tick. Replant it for 75 when it's empty.
06
Spoils
Flatten an enemy building and bank half of what it cost.
07
Every HQ costs more
400, then 500, 625, 781: each one costs 25% more than the last.
08
A slower Bell Tower
Fires every 3 ticks and costs 150. The Campanology class gets it to every 2.
09
Tougher buildings
Each construction class adds +2 armor to your buildings, up from +1.
10
Snow Mobiles sooner
Welding Technology costs 100 and takes 30 ticks. Shells splash half damage on the 8 cells around.
11
Score what you own
At the clock: destruction + survival + grubs in the bank. Grubs dug only break ties.
API
Same commands
Still /api/v1. Read each match's numbers from GET /matches/{id}/rules.
Try them now: pick Spring 2027 under Rules when you start a match. They become the default after the next regular season match.
