BADGER BRAWL · SPRING 2027

The new rules, through a game

Try them now: pick Spring 2027 under Rules when you start a match.
1
Dig
Two dig per cell. Two workers dig from each cell beside a patch; two more wait their turn.
Patches grow back, faster when fuller, never past their start. Dug to 0, gone.
Compost Heaps. Build 100, grows 300 grubs, dug 1 a tick. Replant for 75.
2
Build
Every HQ costs 25% more than the last: 400, 500, 625, 781.
Slower Bell Tower: every 3 ticks, 150 grubs. Campanology gets it to every 2.
Tougher buildings: each construction class adds +2 armor.
3
Fight
A cell holds 9 points: Worker 2, Soldier 1, Archer 1, Scout 2, Snow Mobile 9. First in keeps it.
Spoils: flatten a building, bank half its cost.
Snow Mobiles sooner: Welding Technology 100, 30 ticks. Shells splash half damage around them.
4
The clock
Nobody out by the tick limit? The highest score wins, and the score counts what you own:
Destruction
+ Survival
+ Grubs in the bank
Grubs you dug only break ties.
Same API and commands: read each match's numbers from GET /matches/{id}/rules. Spring rules become the default after the next regular season match.
